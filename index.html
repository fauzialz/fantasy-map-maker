M map.byfauzi.com How it works

Draw a world that looks like it came out of a book

A fantasy map editor that runs in your browser. Paint a coastline and the concentric wave rings derive themselves; scatter mountains and woodland; or generate a whole world and then edit every piece of it.

Start a map No account, nothing to install — your maps stay on your device.

The rings draw themselves

Paint a coast and the concentric wave bands are derived from the land itself — around islands, around lakes, and merging into one band where a strait pinches two shores together.

Paint the land, don't place tiles

The brush adds area. Overlapping strokes merge into a single landmass, the sea brush can cut one in two, and a lake is simply a hole you left behind.

Or generate a world, then edit it

Noise becomes coastlines, biomes, mountains and woodland in a fraction of a second — and all of it is ordinary geometry you can move, recolour or delete. Nothing is locked.

Everything is an object

Drag a continent and the mountains standing on it come with it. Select across layers, resize, restack, rename, delete — and undo any of it in one step.

Export what you make

PNG, JPG or WebP, at up to four times the canvas — drawn through the same renderer as the screen, so an export cannot drift from the map you were looking at. Every picture on this page came out of it.

Free, and specifically how

Everything that runs in your browser is free: every tool, every export format, and as many maps as your device will hold. There is no account to make, and the editor never asks for one.
